Into Frame/Viewer

Controls

Look
Drag with the mouse or a finger
Move
W A S D or the arrow keys; the pad at the bottom left on touch screens. You can walk a few metres around where the photograph was taken.
Motion
Space pauses, M turns motion off, R returns to the photograph's view
VR
With a headset: Enter VR; left stick walks, right stick turns in 30° steps

The view opens where the photograph was taken. Everything outside its frame was generated.
